What's new in 5.1

A steadier swipe, and theming that keeps everything you styled.

Lock popover size while swiping

A new toggle in the Popover tab keeps the popover at one size as you swipe through a Pop Group, instead of resizing to fit each Pop. Turn it on when you want the popover to hold still under the cursor; leave it off to keep the per-Pop fit.

Theming fixes

Saved themes now capture your app-label font size and your Dock Icon styling, so a theme restores the whole look and not just part of it. The Glass Effect slider also shows up on the default background, where it was previously hidden.

Plus fixes and polish throughout.
