What's new in 5.5

This update adds MacOS27 support on top of the following improvements:

  • New: Redesigned app drawer makes it easier to find apps for your Pops
  • New: List View and Tile view added along side the traditional icon grid view
  • New: Theme manager interface makes making and managing themes easier.
  • New: Pop Border options
  • Updated: Materials and Liquid Glass rendering to make Pops more beautiful
  • Redesigned sidebar to make customizing your pops more fun and intuitive

Plus fixes and polish throughout.
