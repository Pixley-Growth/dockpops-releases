What's new in 4.2

DockPops 4.2 introduces bug fixes and Quick Look support when browsing files and folders.

Bigger Pops

Premium now supports up to 20 Pops with 25 items each.

Menu Bar mode

Run DockPops from the menu bar instead of the Dock. Choose whether a click opens your Pop carousel or a quick menu.

Multiple Dock Icon improvements

Companion app Dock Icons no longer stay open or take up Cmd+Tab space. Add, move, or remove items from Pops without entering the Organizer window.

Folder browsing

Open folders inside a Pop and browse their contents without jumping to Finder.

Quick Look

Press Space over files or folders to open a file preview.

Keyboard navigation

Use the arrow keys to select items, Enter to open them, Space for a preview, and Esc to go back or close a Pop.

Now in German and Spanish

Menus, settings, and the organizer speak your language.

Built-in help

A complete help guide, right inside the app.

Plus a clearer Multiple Dock Icons setup, a tidier page control when you have lots of Pops, a faster organizer, smoother swipes, menu bar and per-Pop Dock icon fixes, and dozens of refinements throughout.
