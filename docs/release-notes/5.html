What's new in 5

DockPops 5 makes your Pops beautiful — and lets you fly between them.

Pop Groups

Group related Pops behind a single Dock icon and swipe through just those — one icon per project, mood, or context, instead of a Dock full of them.

Quick switcher

Jump straight to any Pop from a new inline menu — no more swiping to find the one you want.

Deep theming

Restyle a Pop top to bottom — and save the whole look as your own theme. Start from a dozen built-in themes (Sunset, Ocean, Matrix, and more), then make it yours: backgrounds in color, gradient, or your own photo with a Glass Effect slider; single-color or gradient borders; your own app-label font, size, weight, and color; and Dock icons that match the theme or get their own look.

Smarter suggestions

On-device Apple Intelligence now grounds its picks in each Pop's category, so the apps it suggests actually fit (requires macOS 26).

Plus complete German and Latin American Spanish, and fixes and polish throughout.
