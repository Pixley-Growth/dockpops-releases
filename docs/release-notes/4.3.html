What's new in 4.3

DockPops 4.3 lets you fine-tune how the popover looks, adds new hover and press feedback, and fixes the organizer window.

Customize your popover

Settings now split into Popover and Popover Icons. Choose your icon labels (Always, On hover, or Off), label size, popover size (now free for everyone), and grid spacing (Compact, Comfortable, or Spacious).

Hover highlight

Pick how strongly an icon highlights when you point at it — None, Subtle, or Heavy. Icons now also darken when you press them, like a real button.

Popover position

Nudge the popover farther from or closer to the Dock to clear your Dock's hover labels — with a live preview as you drag.

Fixes

Fixed the organizer window clipping its columns, and removed a stray menu item that shouldn't have appeared.

German and Spanish

The new settings are translated into German and Latin American Spanish.
