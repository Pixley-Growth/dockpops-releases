What's new in 6.0

DockPops 6.0 brings your Pops to life — and lets you restyle them just by asking.

Change a Pop by just asking

Tell the new Assistant what you want — “make it feel like a walk in the forest,” “add Safari,” “switch to list view” — and it changes the selected Pop while you watch. Every change is listed, and Undo takes it back. (Premium, macOS 27)

PopFX live backgrounds

A new kind of background that moves and responds to your pointer: arcs, curtains, waves, marble, snow, embers, falling code, and more. There are now 23 built-in themes, 11 of them PopFX.

A redesigned UI

DockPops 6.0 makes the customization controls friendlier and easier to use. The new App drawer makes it easier to find apps for your Pop, and the redesigned inspector allows you to control every aspect of how your Pop looks.

A keyboard shortcut for each Pop

Give any Pop a Control-Option shortcut and open it at your pointer from any app. (Premium)

Add Files…

Add files and folders to a Pop straight from the setup window — or press Shift-Command-F. (Premium)

Show with this icon

On the Dock tab, choose which Pops you can swipe to from a Pop's own Dock icon.

More accessible

Clearer VoiceOver labels and announcements, more of the setup window within reach of the keyboard, and Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency respected throughout.

Plus, Pop limit has been increased from 20 to 100, an updated Help book, and fixes and polish throughout.
